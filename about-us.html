About Us

Your Trusted Travel Companion Across Jammu, Kashmir & Ladakh

Reliable & Professional Service

Moosa Transporter provides safe, comfortable and hassle-free transportation for tourists, families, corporates and adventure travellers. With deep knowledge of local routes and weather conditions, we ensure every journey is smooth and memorable.

Diverse Fleet Options

From Sedans and SUVs to Innova Crysta, Travellers and Luxury Buses — our fleet is well-maintained and suitable for all terrains across Kashmir and Ladakh. Whether it's sightseeing, airport pickup, or long routes, we have the perfect vehicle for your needs.

24/7 Support & Custom Travel Plans

We offer personalised itineraries, transparent pricing (no hidden charges), and round-the-clock customer support to make your travel seamless. Explore Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonmarg, Kargil, Leh, Nubra Valley and more with full confidence.
